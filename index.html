I study how AI systems understand the world — how they perceive, reason, and interact with complex environments.

My research focuses on embodied AI, multimodal learning, and trustworthy AI. I want to build systems that learn from many senses, not just text, and that are dependable enough to genuinely help people day to day.

Pushing the boundaries of what AI can do excites me — but I believe technical progress should go hand in hand with thinking about its impact on people. I want to be a researcher who advances the field and takes responsibility for how that work shapes the world.
