Efficient Visual Question Answering under a 3B Parameter Budget

Yookyung Youn · Korea University

Which parts of a vision–language model can be removed?

The challenge was to answer multiple-choice questions about images while loading fewer than three billion parameters. I started with InstructBLIP-Flan-T5-XL, a 4.023B-parameter model. Early encoder experiments degraded performance, so I concentrated the compression on the language decoder.

Making room under the limit

I removed feed-forward neurons and attention heads, then used the original model as a teacher to stabilize the smaller decoder. I implemented LoRA adapters for the resized layers and fine-tuned them with the backbone frozen.

How much answer accuracy returned?

Competition submission stagePublic weighted accuracy
Structured pruning82.83%
After knowledge distillation85.73%
After LoRA fine-tuning86.59%

Cumulative stages on the same public competition metric. The final model gained 3.76 percentage points over the pruned submission.

What the leaderboard missed

During development I observed weaker language generation even when the model could still select the correct answer. That qualitative observation raised a question the competition score could not resolve: which capabilities survive compression when the evaluation only checks the selected option?
